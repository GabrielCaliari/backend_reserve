# Product Overview

API PPPI is a comprehensive business intelligence platform focused on Brazilian company data and brand monitoring. The system provides:

## Core Features
- **Company Data Management**: CNPJ-based company information lookup and analysis
- **Brand Monitoring**: INPI trademark monitoring and alert system
- **Journal Processing**: Automated processing of official Brazilian business journals
- **AI-Powered Analysis**: Brand analysis using AI models (OpenAI/Azure)
- **Lead Management**: CRM functionality with lead qualification and email campaigns
- **Export Services**: Company data export with filtering capabilities
- **Subscription Management**: Stripe-integrated billing and plan management

## Key Business Domains
- Brazilian company registry (CNPJ) data processing
- Trademark and intellectual property monitoring
- Business intelligence and market analysis
- Automated notification systems
- Customer relationship management

## Target Users
- Business analysts researching Brazilian companies
- Legal professionals monitoring trademark registrations
- Marketing teams conducting market research
- Entrepreneurs validating business opportunities
